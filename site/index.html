Fable Projects

Small tools for sewing. Nothing to install; nothing leaves your browser.

Thimble

Cutting-table calculators: bias strips, circle skirts, gathers, pleats, buttons, elastic, knit bands, waistbands, sleeve cap ease, cup size and FBA, fabric width, shrinkage, stretch, thread, weighing fabric, price compare, yardage, needle and overlocker settings. Add it to a phone's home screen and it works offline.

Bolt End

Shelf labels, swatch cards and hang tags printed from a Stash export, on Avery sheets or plain card.

The calibration cards for Pattern Projector are files, not pages: calibration/ in the repository.
